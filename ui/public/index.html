Wrap

Move XNT from your wallet into the reserve and receive cXNT 1:1.

One-time 0.39% fee on wrap. Unwrapping and transfers are free. The wrapped amount is public.

Send privately

Transfer cXNT to another confidential account. The amount is encrypted on-chain.

The recipient must have a confidential cXNT account (Create account) and gives you its address. A wallet address also works if that wallet already has one.

Unwrap

Burn cXNT and receive the same amount of XNT from the reserve. No fee.

The unwrapped amount is public.

What is private, and what is not

Hidden: balances and transfer amountsStored on-chain as ciphertext. Only the account owner can decrypt a balance; each transfer carries zero-knowledge proofs that it is valid.
Public: addresses and wrap / unwrap amountsWho holds an account, who sent to whom, and how much enters or leaves the reserve are visible. This is confidentiality of amounts, not anonymity.
Fully backedConfidential tokens are only minted against tokens deposited in the program's reserve, and are burned on unwrap. Supply always equals the reserve.
Self-custodial and permissionlessYour keys are derived in this page from your wallet's signature and never leave it. Anyone can wrap, send and unwrap; no one can freeze or move your balance.
